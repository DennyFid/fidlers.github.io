Marks Cars & Motos

Mark's rolling stock history from the days of delivering papers in the old pedal car to his current project.

1970 Schwinn Bicycle

Classic Bicycle

The quintessential 1970s American neighborhood cruiser. Built with heavy-duty Chicago steel, a banana seat, and high-rise handlebars, ready for cruising the block from morning until the streetlights came on.

Pedal Car

Pedal Power

Mark's very first taste of wheeled independence—the classic red Fire Chief pedal car. Used for delivering papers around the neighborhood and getting early miles behind the wheel before upgrading to horsepower.

Powered by the classic pull-start lawnmower engine that started a generation of motorheads. Loud, fast, raw backyard fun with a centrifugal clutch, rigid frame, and plenty of thrills.

An early pioneer of BMX culture featuring tough composite Mag wheels. Built for neighborhood dirt jumps, curb hops, and unmistakable retro street style.
